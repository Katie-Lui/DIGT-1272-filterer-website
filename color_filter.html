Description
While taking the course Mobilizing Digital Citizenship we learned that there are many ways technology has helped make life more convienent for people with disabilities. With what we learned we understood that there are many people who suffer from visual imparements. This includes color blindness. We have studied different types of color blindness such as red-green color blindness (deuteranomaly, protanomaly, deuteranopia) and blue-yellow color blindness (tritanomaly, tritanopia).
With our research we have learned that emphasizing a certain color can help distinguish between the colors they would usually struggle seeing. Therefore we have created a webpage that allows you to pass a photo into the generator and will place a filter over the image helping emphasize specific colors.
To use our website is simple, you only need to follow 3 simple steps.
  1. Download the image you wish to put a filter over.
  2. Click on the Choose File button and upload your image.
  3. Click either the red-tint filter or the blue-tint filter to view the image with the filter.
And done! Simple as that.
